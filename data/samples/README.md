# Sample set (synthetic)

Generated by `scripts/make_samples.py`. Every company, person, address, part number and price is invented.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `inbox/` | 15 emails as `.eml`: 12 purchase orders and 3 non-orders (an invoice, a quote request, a newsletter) |
| `pdf/` | The 12 order PDFs on their own: clean system PDFs in three layouts, a 4-page fax with a cover sheet and terms, a noisy fax, a sideways scan, an upside-down scan and a handwritten form |
| `truth/` | Ground truth per order: what the document says, and the expected customer, ship-to, review status and flags |
| `mock_responses/` | Hand-written responses in the model's output shape, replayed by the offline demo and the tests. Not real model output |
